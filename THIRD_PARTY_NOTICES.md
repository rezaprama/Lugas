# Third-party notices

Lugas distributes original code, Skill instructions, editorial templates, and
documentation. A citation or conceptual influence does **not** place the cited work
under a Lugas license. No third-party corpus text is included in this release.

This inventory records material considered during design. It is not legal advice.
For machine-readable decisions, see
[`data/sources/source-registry.yaml`](data/sources/source-registry.yaml).

## Excluded source material

### University of Manchester Academic Phrasebank

- Owner/publisher: The University of Manchester.
- References: [resource](https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/),
  [about page](https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/about-academic-phrasebank/),
  and [University copyright notice](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/copyright/).
- Lugas use: high-level conceptual orientation about rhetorical moves only.
- Distribution decision: excluded. No page, PDF, phrase inventory, mirror, or
  source sentence was scraped, imported, transformed, or redistributed.

The University's copyright notice says its web material is protected and may not be
reproduced without permission. Lugas therefore treats the inventory as
all-rights-reserved unless a specific permission says otherwise.

### Supervisor-provided workbook

The local file `Academic_Phrasebank_Sections.xlsx` carried an academic-use-only and
no-redistribution notice. It was inspected locally for aggregate structure and
quality signals. Its wording, formatting, labels, and candidate staging output are
not shipped. Public reports disclose counts only. The file is ignored by version
control and remains outside every release artifact.

### British Academic Written English Corpus (BAWE)

- Authors: Hilary Nesi, Sheena Gardner, Paul Thompson, and Paul Wickens.
- Repository: [Oxford Text Archive record](https://ota.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/repository/xmlui/handle/20.500.12024/2539).
- Stated license: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 Unported.
- Distribution decision: excluded. BAWE was not downloaded or ingested.

The non-commercial and share-alike conditions do not match this project's intended
unrestricted software/data distribution workflow. This is a project policy choice,
not a statement that the license forbids every research use.
