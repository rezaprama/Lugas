# Community code of conduct

Lugas aims to be a rigorous, welcoming international community for researchers,
students, editors, linguists, and developers. Participation means helping others do
careful work without humiliation, gatekeeping, or pressure to compromise integrity.

## Expected conduct

- Critique claims, data, and code; treat people with respect.
- Explain jargon and assumptions when they affect a decision.
- Credit ideas, labour, review, and sources accurately.
- Respect privacy, consent, access restrictions, and embargoes.
- Welcome correction. State uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Make space for different disciplines, English varieties, experience levels, and
  accessibility needs.
- Stop or redirect work that could fabricate evidence, erase attribution, expose a
  manuscript, or misuse restricted material.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, threats, stalking, sexual attention, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information or manuscript without permission.
- Plagiarism, false authorship, fabricated evidence, deceptive benchmark reporting,
  or concealment of provenance.
- Pressuring contributors to bypass institutional, publisher, license, or ethics rules.
- Spam, artificial popularity schemes, impersonation, or undisclosed promotion.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

These standards apply in repository spaces, project events, official communications,
and public situations where someone represents Lugas. They also apply to private
interactions when those interactions materially affect community safety.

## Reporting

For immediate danger, contact appropriate local services. For project concerns, report
privately through GitHub's repository contact or private vulnerability-reporting
channel. Do not open a public issue when it would expose personal data, restricted
text, or a target of harassment. Include only the minimum evidence needed.

Maintainers will limit access, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and offer the subject of
a report a chance to respond when safety allows. A maintainer named in a report must
recuse. No process can promise absolute confidentiality on third-party platforms.

## Enforcement

Responses are proportional to impact, pattern, intent, and repair. They may include a
private clarification, content edit, warning, temporary participation limit, removal,
or permanent ban. Urgent protective action may precede full review. Decisions should
be documented privately, consistently applied, and open to one appeal to an uninvolved
maintainer when available.

Good-faith mistakes invite correction. Bad-faith evasion, retaliation, or repeated
harm brings stronger action. Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and
enforcing this policy.
