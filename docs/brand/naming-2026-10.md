# Naming research: RhetoriLex → Lugas

Decision date: 22 October 2026. Decided by the maintainer (Reza Prama Arviandi) from the shortlist below. This record is evidence for an internal decision, not a trademark opinion: an empty registry or search result does not establish rights to a name, and no trademark database was searched.

## Why rename

*RhetoriLex* is hard to say and spell for Indonesian users, and *rhetoric* signals a niche linguistics tool rather than help with paraphrasing and editing a thesis or article.

## Brand vs descriptor

The brand is short and ownable in context; the descriptor carries the search terms.

- **Brand:** Lugas
- **Descriptor (ID):** skill parafrase dan penyuntingan naskah ilmiah yang setia makna
- **Descriptor (EN):** meaning-safe academic paraphrase and editing skill
- **Tagline:** *Tulisan jernih, makna tetap.* / *Clear writing. Same meaning.*
- **One sentence:** Lugas helps you paraphrase, edit, and translate Indonesian and English academic writing without changing numbers, citations, negation, or claim strength.

In Indonesian, *lugas* describes language that is plain, straightforward, and to the point (see the entry in [KBBI daring](https://kbbi.kemdikbud.go.id/entri/lugas)); *bahasa yang lugas* is a common editorial ideal for scholarly prose.

## Internal decision rubric (/100)

A transparent weighting for this decision only. It is not a market, SEO, GEO, or branding score.

| Criterion | Weight | What earns points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Say and spell in ID and EN | 20 | one obvious pronunciation in both languages; no silent or ambiguous letters |
| Short and memorable | 15 | ≤ 6 letters, two syllables |
| Fits the function | 20 | meaning relates to clear, faithful writing |
| Distinct, low conflict | 25 | no same-class product, free package names, low noise in search |
| Room to grow | 10 | not tied to one feature (e.g. only paraphrase) |
| Discovery with descriptor | 10 | brand searches are not swamped by an everyday word |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| **Total** | **79** | **76** | **71** |

Lugas loses points on the npm clash (LugasJS) but wins on pronunciation (*LOO-gus* in both languages), a meaning that is a recognised editorial ideal, and fewer everyday-word collisions than *tepat*. The PyPI name `lugas` was free on the decision date and is used by this project's `pyproject.toml`; it has not been published to PyPI.

## Uncertainty

- The original 22 October decision did not include a trademark search. A limited PDKI spot-check was added on 24 October; it is not trademark clearance.
- Web search coverage is partial; app-store and social-handle availability were not checked exhaustively.
- Search demand was not measured. No Google Trends data was available to this session; nothing is inferred about volume.
- Registry results can change; recheck before publishing a package.

## PDKI follow-up (24 October 2026)

A public exact-text PDKI search for **Lugas** returned 11 results. The visible results include a registered `Lugas` mark in class 9 (DID2026015767), whose listed goods include measurement/sensor products and some downloadable software. The PDKI phonetic search also lists close spellings such as *LUGAS*, *LGAS*, *LULAS*, and *LUKAS*. This is a potential name conflict worth reviewing; the listed goods are not themselves an academic-writing service, so the database result alone does not settle whether the project can use or register the mark.

This was a limited text/phonetic lookup, not a comprehensive search across relevant goods and service classes, logo marks, WIPO, or USPTO. PDKI warns that its records may lag and should not be the sole basis for a decision; consult a registered intellectual-property consultant before commercial use or paid promotion. The 79/100 internal shortlist score above predates this lookup and is not a market, SEO, GEO, or legal score. See the [PDKI results for Lugas](https://pdki-indonesia.dgip.go.id/search?keyword=Lugas&page=1&show_filter=true&type=trademark) and [DJKI guidance on trademark checks](https://dgip.go.id/index.php/faq/daftar-faq/merek/Merek-Pelayanan%20Teknis).
