Asosiasi dan kausalitas

Tautan tetap

Panduan asosiasi dan kausalitas agar bahasa ilmiah tetap sebanding dengan rancangan, bukti, ketidakpastian, dan cakupan.

Contoh orisinal

Income and tree cover were associated in the survey; the analysis does not establish that income caused the observed difference.

Tujuan retoris
Distinguish observed covariation from evidence that changing one factor changes another.
Kegunaan
  • results interpretation
  • abstracts
  • press-facing summaries
Fungsi frasa terkait
distinguish_causation, describe_association
Skill terkait
Bahasa korelasi
Peringatan bukti dan klaim
Causal verbs require a credible causal design; association, prediction, significance, and null results do not establish causation.

Cakupan dan generalisasi

Tautan tetap

Panduan cakupan dan generalisasi agar bahasa ilmiah tetap sebanding dengan rancangan, bukti, ketidakpastian, dan cakupan.

Contoh orisinal

Limit conclusions to populations, settings, outcomes, and time horizons supported by the study.
