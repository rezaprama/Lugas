Clear writing. Same meaning.

Lugas is a free paraphrase and editing skill for theses, journal articles, and technical reports in Indonesian and English. A local meaning check flags changed numbers, citations, negation, and claims, and your manuscript never goes to our server.

Interface language and pattern language are separate. Phrase patterns remain in English.

Style follows evidence

A fluent sentence can still misstate the study. Calibrate the relation before polishing the prose.

Paraphrase that drifts

Okafor and Lindqvist (2019) showed that retrofitting reduces bridge defects across all districts.

Paraphrase that keeps the evidence

In a single-district sample of 46 retrofitted bridges, Okafor and Lindqvist (2019) observed an association with fewer defects reported at inspection.
