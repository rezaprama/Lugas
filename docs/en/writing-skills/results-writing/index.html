Introducing results

Permanent link

Practical guidance to orient the reader to the analysis or outcome being reported before presenting values, with wording aligned to rhetorical purpose and available evidence.

Original example

Rhetorical objective
Orient the reader to the analysis or outcome being reported before presenting values.

Make every estimate interpretable

Results need a reference frame: outcome, comparison, magnitude, direction, unit, time, population, and uncertainty. Text should guide readers to the findings that answer the question. Read Results Writing.

Inference boundary

Statistical significance does not replace an estimate, and a non-significant result does not prove no effect. Report direct comparisons and compatible values.
