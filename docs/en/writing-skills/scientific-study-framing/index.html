Scientific background

Permanent link

Practical guidance to summarise the established scientific conditions needed to understand the study, with wording aligned to rhetorical purpose and available evidence.

Original example

Seasonal oxygen depletion alters nutrient cycling in many shallow freshwater systems.

Rhetorical objective
Summarise the established scientific conditions needed to understand the study.

  • registered reports

Frame the estimand, not only the topic

Scientific framing should identify what quantity or relation the study aims to learn, in which population, under which comparison, and over what time. The design must fit that target. Use Scientific Writing for section-level guidance.

Design boundary

A hypothesis does not create identification. State design assumptions and avoid causal objectives when the study can estimate association only.
