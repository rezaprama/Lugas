Academic abstract

Permanent link

Practical guidance to condense the problem, approach, principal result, and bounded conclusion into a self-contained summary, with wording aligned to rhetorical purpose and available evidence.

Original example

Using panel data from 42 districts, we estimate changes in clinic attendance and find substantial variation by travel time.

Rhetorical objective
Condense the problem, approach, principal result, and bounded conclusion into a self-contained summary.

Compress without changing the paper

Titles, abstracts, cover letters, and responses need concise statements of design, finding, contribution, and limits. Compression must preserve claim strength. Read Reviewer Response for revision language.

Integrity boundary

Do not claim analyses, citations, approvals, novelty, reporting compliance, or revisions that are absent from the submitted files.
