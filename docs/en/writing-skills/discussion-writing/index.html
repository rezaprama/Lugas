Summarising principal findings

Permanent link

Practical guidance to restate the main answer at the start of the discussion without repeating every result, with wording aligned to rhetorical purpose and available evidence.

Original example

The principal finding is that service continuity improved, but gains varied substantially across districts.

Rhetorical objective
Restate the main answer at the start of the discussion without repeating every result.

Evidence and claim warning
Separate results from interpretation, label explanations as provisional, and bound implications by design and uncertainty.

Separate answer, explanation, and implication

Begin with the supported answer. Compare prior evidence on a stated basis, mark explanations as tested or possible, and connect limitations to their consequences. Read Discussion Writing.

Interpretation boundary

Interest, novelty, and narrative importance do not justify stronger language. Generalisation and causal claims require their own evidence.
