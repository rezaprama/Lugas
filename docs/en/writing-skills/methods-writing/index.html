Study design

Permanent link

Practical guidance to name the design and connect it to the unit, timing, comparison, and inferential goal, with wording aligned to rhetorical purpose and available evidence.

Original example

We conducted a preregistered, parallel-group experiment with measurements at baseline and four weeks.

Rhetorical objective
Name the design and connect it to the unit, timing, comparison, and inferential goal.

Make choices and sequence traceable

Methods writing should connect design, population, measures, procedures, exclusions, and models to the target analysis. Name prespecified and data-informed decisions. Read Methods Writing for a reproducibility audit.

Reporting boundary

Do not hide subjective decisions in passive voice or label work reproducible without providing the detail and materials that the claim requires.
