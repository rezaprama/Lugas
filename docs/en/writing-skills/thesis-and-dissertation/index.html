Thesis introduction

Permanent link

Practical guidance to open the thesis by connecting context, problem, aim, approach, and contribution, with wording aligned to rhetorical purpose and available evidence.

Original example

This thesis investigates how coastal households adapt water use during recurring dry seasons.

Rhetorical objective
Open the thesis by connecting context, problem, aim, approach, and contribution.
Use cases
  • theses
  • dissertations
  • capstone reports
Related phrase functions
establish_context, state_contribution

Keep the document spine visible

The problem, question, design, findings, and bounded contribution should remain aligned across the abstract, chapters, and conclusion. Chapter signposting is useful when it states a logical function. Read Thesis Writing for a cross-chapter audit.

Contribution boundary

Do not inflate a local methodological or empirical contribution into novelty for an entire field. State who can use the contribution and under which conditions.
