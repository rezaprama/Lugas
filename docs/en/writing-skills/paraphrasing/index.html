Meaning-preserving paraphrase

Permanent link

Practical guidance to restate a passage in independent language while preserving its propositions, scope, and attribution, with wording aligned to rhetorical purpose and available evidence.

Original example

Source: The intervention produced a modest decline in waiting time. Rewrite: Waiting time decreased modestly following the intervention.

Rhetorical objective
Restate a passage in independent language while preserving its propositions, scope, and attribution.

Treat paraphrase as constrained rewriting

Identify protected elements before changing structure: citations, numbers, units, intervals, negation, comparison direction, technical terms, population, time, uncertainty, and causal status. Read Paraphrasing for the full workflow.

Meaning boundary

Surface difference does not prove originality or fidelity. Verify that the source entails the rewrite and that the rewrite adds no unsupported proposition.
