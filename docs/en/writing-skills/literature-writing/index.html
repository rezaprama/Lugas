Make the comparison basis explicit

Literature writing depends on why sources belong together. Group studies by question, theory, design, population, measure, finding, or limitation. Then state whether evidence agrees, differs, or cannot be compared. Read Literature Review for a synthesis workflow.

Citation boundary

Every cited study must support the relation attributed to it. Several citations do not automatically establish consensus, quality, or a research gap.
